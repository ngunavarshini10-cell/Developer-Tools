Dr. A.P.J Abdul kalam

Dr.A.P.J kalam was one of india's greatest scientists and the 11th president of india.kalam was born and raised in a muslim family in rameswaram,tamilnadu,kalam studied physics and aerospace engineering.

He is popularly known as the "Missile man of india" for his work on the development of ballistic missile and launch vehicle technology.Dr.kalam believed that education,hardwork,and determination could help anyone achieve success.

His resarch and leadership brought him recognition in the 1980s,which prompted the government to initiate an advanced missile programme under his directorship.His simple lifestyle,honesty,and dedication to the nation made him a true role model.
